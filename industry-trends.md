# 2026 Industry Trends Integration

To ensure the program remains future-proof, the following trends were integrated into the decision-making process:

### 1. Continuous Compliance (Real-time Audit)
Moving away from annual audits to 24/7 monitoring using SQL-based auditing and automated Linux compliance checks.

### 2. Supply Chain & Data Logistics
Applying logistics expertise to secure the "Software Bill of Materials" (SBOM) and ensure data integrity across all operational nodes.

### 3. Cyber-Criminology
Leveraging criminal psychology to understand adversary motivations, allowing for predictive defense rather than just reactive patching.

### 4. GenAI for IT Ops
Utilizing AI to summarize complex logs and automate the "self-healing" of misconfigured systems.
