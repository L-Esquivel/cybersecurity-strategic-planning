# AI Collaborative Session Log: The "Making Of"

This document records the iterative process and the "Human + AI" collaboration that resulted in the strategic plan. It highlights the use of specialized prompts to bridge the gap between different professional domains.

## Phase 1: Idea Generation
*   **Prompt Strategy:** Simulating a business environment where the AI acts as a client looking for protection.
*   **Result:** A comprehensive list of services ranging from technical hardening to behavioral analysis based on my background in Criminology and Logistics.

## Phase 2: Refinement & Analysis
*   **Prompt Strategy:** Requesting a cost-benefit matrix to filter "expensive but low-impact" ideas and prioritize high-value operational tasks.
*   **Result:** Identification of "Automated Compliance" and "Linux Hardening" as the most viable high-impact projects.

## Phase 3: Trend Integration
*   **Prompt Strategy:** Contextualizing the plan within 2026 cybersecurity trends to ensure the program is future-proof.
*   **Result:** Strategic alignment with GenAI Ops, Supply Chain Integrity, and Ciber-Criminology.
