# Lab 5: Cybersecurity Strategic Planning & AI-Driven Brainstorming

## 📌 Overview
This repository documents the strategic phase of developing a cybersecurity program. The goal was to utilize AI as a collaborative partner to identify high-impact services, evaluate their feasibility, and align them with current industry trends.

By combining my background in **Criminology** and **Logistics** with **IT Operations**, I have defined a roadmap for security solutions that are not only technically sound but also operationally efficient.

## 🚀 Key Objectives
*   Identify essential cybersecurity services for modern organizations.
*   Conduct a Cost-Benefit analysis for prioritized implementation.
*   Align the program with 2026 industry trends (GenAI, Supply Chain Security, and Continuous Compliance).

## 🛠️ Methodology
Used AI-driven brainstorming sessions to simulate a business environment, acting as both the service provider and the client to refine the program's value proposition.
